## What keeps it light

- **No client-side framework.** Pages are static HTML generated by [Zola](https://www.getzola.org). The only JavaScript is a few small scripts from the theme: search, theme switcher, copy buttons.
- **Fonts cut down to what the site uses.** Inter and Source Serif are subset to Latin: 198 kB instead of 411 kB, and only articles load the serif.
- **HTML served from the edge.** CloudFront keeps every page at the point of presence nearest to the reader and serves it compressed (Brotli or gzip), over HTTP/2 or HTTP/3. Each deploy purges that cache.
- **The next page is fetched before the click.** When a link is hovered, the browser prefetches its page ([Speculation Rules](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Speculation_Rules_API)).

## What these numbers don't say

They come from a lab. Lighthouse emulates a mid-range phone on a slow 4G connection, on a GitHub Actions runner, against a local copy of the site. They describe how the pages are built, not what a given reader experiences: real devices, networks and caches vary, and the trip to the CDN is not in them. Analytics (Umami) and comments (giscus) are left out of the runs.
